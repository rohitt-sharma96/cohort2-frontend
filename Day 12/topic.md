## position: absolute

- jo bhi element absolute hota hai wo uth jaata hai and agar tumne us element ko move kiya to wo kisi ke upar bhi aa skta hai

- koi bhi element par absolute lagaane se wo upar uth jaata hai, uski space khaali hoti hai and uski jagah baad waala element aa jataa hai

- top left bottom right and z-inde ye sirf kaam krti hai jab aapka element ke paas koi position ki value ho jaise ki
        e.g. - absolute, relative, sticky, fixed
