Units -

1. px - pexels (its size is fixed)
2. % - percent (it size is depend on parent)
3. vw - viewport width ( depends of viewport's width)
4. vh - viewport height ( depends of viewport's height)
5. vmax - viewport maximum (depends of maximum)
6. vmin - viewport minimum (depends of minimum)
7. em - according to the parent and can be controlled by parent.
8. rem - according to the root and can be controlled by root.
